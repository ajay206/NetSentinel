| # | Area | Finding | Fix |

Found by hand in earlier steps of this series and already fixed:

## Rejected

- **"Sparklines fetch 1800 points"**: wrong. It's 30 minutes at a 60 s step, so 30 points per series.
- **"PII filter on `llm.prompt` hurts debugging"**: intended. Prompts belong in Langfuse (self-hosted, opt-in), not in generic traces.
- **"`/metrics` exposed externally"**: not the case. Port 8000 is bound to localhost, and the console's nginx and the k3d ingress only route `/api`, `/health` and `/ws`. Documented in `03-services.md`.
- **"Gateway lacks backend latency logging"**: W22 adds per-service latency metrics and a `trace_id` request log line.
- **"No obs instructions for 8 GB"**: `03-services.md` (W22 section) and `06-troubleshooting.md` cover them, including memory limits.
- **Naming style (kebab-case vs env-var underscores, Makefile targets)**: these are normal conventions, not inconsistencies.
- **Items citing files that don't exist** (`tests/test_smoke.py`, `openapi/*.yaml`) or giving no evidence (Dockerfile "unnecessary dependencies", a k3d eviction threshold "not suitable"): no change.
