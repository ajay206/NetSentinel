**Reading the numbers**

- Compression = alarms / incidents. Root match = share of faults whose main incident has one end of the cut link as root.
- The small topology has few hops between sites, so simultaneous cuts often fall within one relation and merge (Merged). This is a known limit of time + topology clustering; Phase 2 RCA should split using link-path evidence.
- Noise absorbed = single warning alarms close in time and topology to a fault.
