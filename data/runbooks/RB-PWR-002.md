# Generator failed to start

> Synthetic runbook for the NetSentinel demo. Not vendor guidance.

## Symptoms

Mains failure and generator fail-to-start alarm; batteries draining.

## Diagnosis

Check genset controller alarms, fuel level and starter battery.

## Remediation

Send a technician to manually start or refuel the generator; escalate if autonomy is under one hour.

## Escalation

If not resolved within the SLA, escalate to the tier-2 power failure team and attach alarm IDs and KPI charts.
