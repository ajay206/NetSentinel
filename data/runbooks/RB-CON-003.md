# Event crowd capacity surge

> Synthetic runbook for the NetSentinel demo. Not vendor guidance.

## Symptoms

Sudden traffic surge around a venue during an event; RRC setup failures due to admission control.

## Diagnosis

Correlate with the events calendar; check admission rejections.

## Remediation

Activate temporary capacity (cell on wheels); adjust admission thresholds.

## Escalation

If not resolved within the SLA, escalate to the tier-2 congestion team and attach alarm IDs and KPI charts.
