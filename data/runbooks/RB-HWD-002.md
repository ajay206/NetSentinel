# Fan failure and rising temperature

> Synthetic runbook for the NetSentinel demo. Not vendor guidance.

## Symptoms

Fan tray failure alarm; chassis temperature creeping up.

## Diagnosis

Check fan speed readings and inlet temperature.

## Remediation

Replace the fan tray; check air filters.

If not resolved within the SLA, escalate to the tier-2 hw drift team and attach alarm IDs and KPI charts.
