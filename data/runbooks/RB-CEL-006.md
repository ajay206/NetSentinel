# License expiry takes cells off-air

> Synthetic runbook for the NetSentinel demo. Not vendor guidance.

## Symptoms

Cells locked after a capacity license expired; license alarm on the node.

## Diagnosis

Check license status and expiry date on the node.

## Remediation

Install the renewed license key and unlock the cells.

## Escalation

If not resolved within the SLA, escalate to the tier-2 cell outage team and attach alarm IDs and KPI charts.
